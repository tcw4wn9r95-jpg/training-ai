ⓘ What do these mean?
TSB = Fitness (CTL, 42-day avg) − Fatigue (ATL, 7-day avg). Zones (Coggan/Friel): −10 to −30 = productive training; −10 to +10 = maintaining; +10 to +25 = race fresh; above +25 = transition (detraining); below −30 = high risk. Important: TSB only means something while you're training — with no recent load it drifts to 0 while fitness decays, so the app shows Detraining instead.

Where the reference numbers come from
Energy — Mifflin-St Jeor resting energy equation (the predictive equation recommended by the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics).
Protein — 1.2–2.0 g/kg/day for active adults, ACSM / Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics / Dietitians of Canada joint position stand on Nutrition and Athletic Performance (2016); RDA floor 0.8 g/kg (IOM / NASEM Dietary Reference Intakes).
Carbohydrate — 3–5 g/kg light days, 5–7 g/kg moderate (~1 h/day), 6–10 g/kg endurance (1–3 h/day), same joint position stand.
Fat — 20–35% of energy (IOM / NASEM Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Range).
Fibre — 14 g per 1000 kcal (IOM / NASEM Adequate Intake).
Water — EFSA adequate intake for total water, 2.5 L/day men and 2.0 L/day women, of which roughly 20–30% comes from food; plus 0.4–0.8 L per hour of training (ACSM exercise and fluid replacement position stand).
These are population reference values, not medical advice. Your doctor's numbers always win.
