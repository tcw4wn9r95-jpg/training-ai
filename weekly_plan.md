## MONDAY — Easy Aerobic Run

**Sport:** Running
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 18 | **Focus:** Aerobic primer, Zone 1–2, get the legs turning

### Warm-up
- **5 min | Z1 walk/jog — >7:00/km, <145 bpm**
- Start walking briskly, transition to a slow jog. Don't rush this. If HR climbs above 145, walk.

### Main Set
- **20 min | Z2 easy run — 6:15–6:45/km, 146–162 bpm**
- Conversational pace. If you can't speak in full sentences, slow down. This is not supposed to feel like training. It should feel like a pleasant morning out.

### Cool-down
- **5 min | Z1 walk — >7:00/km, <140 bpm**
- Walk it out, let the HR drop fully before stopping.

**Coach notes:** Monday is about starting the week with a win, not a workout. The pace target is a guide — if conditions are hot or you're stiff, go slower and trust the HR ceiling. Zero ego today.

## TUESDAY — Sweet-Spot Indoor Ride

**Sport:** Cycling (indoor)
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 22 | **Focus:** First structured stimulus — introduce upper Zone 3 / low Zone 4 effort

### Warm-up
- **8 min | Z1–Z2 spin — 88–141W**
- Start at 88–100W, gradually build to 130–141W over 8 minutes. Legs should feel warm and smooth by the end. Cadence 85–95 rpm throughout.

### Main Set
- **2 × 6 min @ 155–168W (upper Z3 / sweet-spot) | 3 min easy spin @ 88–100W between reps**
- This is the session's purpose. 155–168W sits just below threshold — aerobically demanding but sustainable. HR will likely sit 163–172 bpm by the end of each rep. That's exactly where we want it. Cadence 88–92 rpm. If your power drops or legs seize, reduce to 145W — don't abandon the interval, just scale it.

### Cool-down
- **7 min | Z1 easy spin — <106W**
- Spin the legs out fully. No stopping abruptly.

**Coach notes:** Two reps is intentionally modest. The point is that you hit both, feel controlled, and finish thinking "I could have done a third." That's the right feeling for a Build week opener. If HR shoots above 175 in the first minute, back off 10–15W and hold there.

## WEDNESDAY — Lower Body & Core Strength

**Sport:** Strength
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 15 | **Focus:** Lower-body strength endurance and core stability — shoulder-free

### Warm-up
- **5 min — Mobility and activation**
- Leg swings (10 each direction, each leg), hip circles (10 each), bodyweight glute bridges (15 reps slow), bodyweight squats (10 reps). Keep it unhurried.

### Main Set
- **2 rounds of the following circuit, 45–60 sec rest between exercises, 90 sec between rounds:**

| Exercise | Sets/Reps | Load | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat | 10 reps | 16 kg | Controlled descent, full depth |
| Romanian Deadlift | 10 reps | 16 kg | Hinge from hips, soft knees |
| TRX Split Squat | 8 reps each leg | Bodyweight | Rear foot in TRX, front foot flat |
| Band Glute Bridge | 15 reps | Light band above knees | Pause 2 sec at top |
| Dead Bug | 8 reps each side | Bodyweight | Slow, breathe out on extension |
| Plank | 30 sec hold | Bodyweight | Brace core, neutral spine |

### Cool-down
- **5 min — Static stretching**
- Hip flexor stretch (30s each), seated hamstring stretch (30s each), pigeon pose or figure-4 glute stretch (30s each). Breathe slowly into each stretch.

**Coach notes:** Two rounds only this week — resist the urge to add a third. The load is 16 kg across the board, stepping down from what was available because we're building consistency, not chasing soreness. Wednesday's goal is to arrive at Thursday's ride with functional legs, not wrecked ones. Shoulder stays completely passive throughout — no overhead, no pressing, no pulling through that joint.

## THURSDAY — Long Aerobic Ride

**Sport:** Cycling (indoor or outdoor — your choice)
**Duration:** 90 min | **Planned TSS:** 52 | **Focus:** Extended Zone 2 aerobic development — the week's anchor session

### Warm-up
- **10 min | Z1 easy — <106W (indoor) / <145 bpm (outdoor)**
- Gradual build from easy spinning. Don't rush into Zone 2. Let the body settle into the effort over these first 10 minutes.

### Main Set
- **70 min | Z2 steady — 106–141W (indoor) / 146–162 bpm (outdoor)**
- This is the session that earns you the week. Long, unbroken, genuinely aerobic effort. The pace of conversation should be possible but not comfortable. Indoors: keep power between 106–141W and cadence 85–95 rpm. Outdoors: ride to HR and ignore terrain — go easy uphill, easy downhill, let the HR guide you. No heroics.

### Cool-down
- **10 min | Z1 easy — <106W / <145 bpm**
- Spin down fully. If outdoors, find a flat section to wind down gradually.

**Coach notes:** Ninety minutes of Zone 2 is where real aerobic adaptation happens — mitochondrial density, fat oxidation, cardiac stroke volume. This session is doing more physiological work than anything else this week, precisely because it's boring and steady. If you feel an urge to push harder, that's the signal to hold back. Trust the zone.
