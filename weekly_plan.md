## Monday 6 July — Aerobic Base Ride

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 18 | **Focus:** Aerobic base development — sustained Z2 power to build mitochondrial density and fat oxidation without stressing the knees beyond low-load pedalling

### Warm-up
- 5 min easy spin at Z1 (70–90W), legs only, relaxed cadence 80–85 rpm. Let the joints wake up before adding load.

### Main set
- 20 min continuous at Z2: **106–130W**, cadence 85–90 rpm, HR should settle 146–158 bpm. Conversational pace — if you can't speak in sentences, back off. Keep power at the lower end (106–120W) if knees feel any anterior discomfort; pedalling should feel smooth, not grindy.

### Cool-down
- 5 min easy spin below 90W, reduce cadence gradually to 70 rpm.

**Coach notes:** This is your aerobic foundation session — the goal is time in Z2, not pushing watts. If the knee flares during pedalling, stop and note exactly where in the pedal stroke it hurts (top of stroke = hip flexor; bottom = patellar). That information is gold for the physio.

## Wednesday 8 July — Knee-Safe Strength (Hip Hinge & Core Focus)

**Sport:** Strength
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 12 | **Focus:** Posterior chain development and anti-rotation core stability — building the glute/hamstring strength that offloads the knee and supports running and cycling economy, with zero spinal flexion under load

### Warm-up
- 3 min: Hip circles (10 each direction), glute bridges bodyweight (15 reps slow), standing band clamshells (15 each side), TRX-assisted squat sit (bodyweight only, shallow — heels elevated if needed, 10 reps).

### Main set
3 rounds, 60s rest between rounds. RIR 2–3 on every set (stop 2–3 reps before failure):

| Exercise | Load | Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Romanian Deadlift (DB)** | 2×11 kg | 10 | Hip hinge only — no knee flexion past 15°. Push hips back, soft knee. Keeps load off patella entirely. |
| **TRX Glute Bridge / Hip Thrust** | Bodyweight | 15 | Feet on floor, shoulders on ground — NOT a low-seat rise. Drive hips to full extension. |
| **Single-leg Hip Hinge (DB)** | 1×6 kg | 8/side | Romanian deadlift on one leg. Balance focus, not weight. |
| **Pallof Press (band)** | Light band | 10/side | Anti-rotation core. Band anchored at door handle height. Press out and hold 2s. |
| **Dead Bug** | Bodyweight | 8/side | Anti-extension core. Lower back FLAT on floor throughout. Slow and controlled. |
| **Standing DB Carry (Farmer carry)** | 2×11 kg | 20m walk | Walk 20m down the hall and back. Core braced, shoulders down. |

### Cool-down
- 5 min static: Hip flexor kneeling stretch (30s/side), lying glute stretch figure-4 (30s/side), hamstring stretch supine (30s/side), thoracic rotation seated (10 reps/side).

**Coach notes:** The Romanian deadlift is your key movement this week — it builds the hamstring-glute chain that reduces anterior knee stress long-term. Keep the weight at 11 kg and earn the form before adding load. If the single-leg hip hinge feels unstable, hold the 4 kg and touch the wall for balance — no shame in that, it's the right call.

**Coach notes:** This is your one run of the week — keep it honest. A 6:30/km pace that feels too fast is too fast. Heart rate is your guide: the moment it climbs above 165, slow down. The goal is 20 minutes of running with zero knee aggravation, full stop.
