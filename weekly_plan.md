**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 18 | **Focus:** Restart aerobic engine at low cost, pure Z2 stimulus

#### Warm-up
- 5 min at Z1 (< 106W). Just pedal. Let the legs remember what this feels like. No HR pressure.

#### Main set
- 20 min steady Z2: **106–130W**, 85–90 rpm. Aim for conversational effort — you should be able to speak in full sentences. HR will likely drift to 146–155 bpm once settled. Don't chase the top of Z2 today; aim for the lower half.

#### Cool-down
- 5 min easy spin, < 100W, let HR drop below 130 bpm before you stop.

**Coach notes:** This session exists purely to say "I'm back." Power targets are soft ceilings — if 110W feels like enough today given the sleep you've had, that is exactly right. Log how your legs feel at minute 10 and minute 20; that feedback tells me more than the numbers.

### Wednesday 17 June — Strength: Lower Body & Core (Shoulder-Safe)

**Sport:** Strength
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 20 | **Focus:** Re-engage legs, glutes, and core without loading the shoulder

#### Warm-up
- 4 min: leg swings (front/back and lateral, 10 each leg), hip circles, 10 bodyweight squats, 10 glute bridges.

#### Main set
**2 rounds** (not 3 — you are coming back from a break on broken sleep):

| Exercise | Reps / Time | Load |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat | 10 reps | 16 kg |
| Romanian Deadlift | 10 reps | 22 kg |
| TRX Split Squat | 8 reps/leg | Bodyweight via TRX |
| Banded Glute Bridge | 15 reps | Band |
| Dead Bug | 6 reps/side | Bodyweight |
| Plank | 30 sec | Bodyweight |

60 sec rest between exercises. 90 sec between rounds.

**Shoulder note:** Dead Bug replaces any pressing or pulling movement. TRX split squat uses the handles for balance only — no load through the shoulder. If the TRX position causes any shoulder discomfort, swap to a reverse lunge holding a single 11 kg dumbbell at your side.

#### Cool-down
- 5 min: hip flexor stretch (60 sec/side), hamstring stretch (45 sec/side), glute figure-4 stretch (45 sec/side).

**Coach notes:** Two rounds is the correct dose this week — not because you can't do three, but because your recovery capacity is compromised. The goal is to stimulate, not accumulate fatigue. We will add the third round next week if sleep improves and this session felt easy.

### Saturday 21 June — Aerobic Builder: Z2 Endurance Ride

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 55 min | **Planned TSS:** 38 | **Focus:** Longest aerobic session of the week — build base durability, primary fat-burning stimulus

#### Warm-up
- 8 min progressive build: start at 90W (Z1), increase 5W every 2 min, arrive at 115W by minute 8.

#### Main set
- **35 min steady Z2: 110–140W**, 87–92 rpm.
  - First 15 min: aim for 110–120W — let the body warm into it.
  - Final 20 min: you can push to 125–140W if HR is stable below 162 bpm and effort feels genuinely sustainable. This is not a test — it is a conversation with your aerobic system.
  - HR reference: 146–162 bpm throughout. If HR climbs above 162 bpm at any power level, back off — this means fatigue/sleep debt is limiting your ceiling today.

#### Cool-down
- 12 min: drop to 90–100W for 5 min, then < 85W for 7 min. Let HR return to < 125 bpm before stopping.

**Coach notes:** This is your anchor session of the week. Thirty-five minutes of quality Z2 at stable power is an excellent aerobic stimulus — it targets fat oxidation, builds mitochondrial density, and does so with minimal recovery cost. Pay attention to how power feels at minute 25–35 versus minute 10 — that perceived effort delta tells us a lot about where your aerobic fitness is right now.
