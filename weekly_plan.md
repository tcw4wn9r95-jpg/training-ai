### Monday — Aerobic Spin Reactivation

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 18 | **Focus:** Restart the aerobic engine, easy and consistent

#### Warm-up
- **10 min @ 88–106W (Z1)** — start slow, legs may feel heavy. Just spin. Cadence 85–90 rpm. No pressure.

#### Main set
- **15 min @ 106–130W (low Z2)** — find a comfortable rhythm. HR should settle around 146–155 bpm. If it climbs above 160, back off the watts. This is a conversation-pace effort on the bike.

#### Cool-down
- **5 min @ <90W (Z1)** — spin easy, let HR drop. Stay seated, relaxed.

**Coach notes:** This is a check-in session as much as a training session. Notice how the legs feel, how HR responds to a given wattage — that data will inform how we load the rest of the week. Don't chase numbers; just complete the time.

### Wednesday — Sweet-Spot Intro (Cycling)

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 25 | **Focus:** First structured effort — introduce sweet-spot stimulus without overcooking a sleep-deprived system

#### Warm-up
- **8 min @ 88–115W (Z1–low Z2)** — gradual build. Start at 88W, nudge up across the 8 minutes. Cadence 85–90 rpm.

#### Main set
- **2 × 8 min @ 142–160W (Z3 sweet-spot, 163–172 bpm)** — this sits just below threshold. Effort should feel "comfortably hard" — you can speak in short sentences, not full ones. 4 min easy spin (88–106W) between intervals.

#### Cool-down
- **6 min @ <90W (Z1)** — easy spin. HR below 145 before you stop.

**Coach notes:** This is your key session of the week on weekdays. Sweet-spot (not full threshold) is the right tool here — it builds FTP-relevant fitness without requiring the nervous system recovery that true Z4 demands. If sleep the night before was terrible (<4h), drop the intervals to 2 × 5 min and reduce watts to 130–142W. Do not white-knuckle threshold work on a broken-sleep day.

**Coach notes:** Thursday is a deliberate deload day between Wednesday's effort and Saturday's longer ride. Resist the temptation to push — the physiological value of this session comes entirely from *not* going hard. Think of it as moving blood through the legs, not training them.

### Saturday — Longer Aerobic Ride (Key Session)

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 60 min | **Planned TSS:** 50 | **Focus:** Build aerobic volume, longest session of the week, metabolic base

#### Warm-up
- **10 min @ 88–106W (Z1)** — gradual build across the 10 minutes. Start easy, finish at the top of Z1. Cadence 85–90 rpm. Let the body arrive at the session, don't force it.

#### Main set
- **40 min @ 106–141W (Z2 aerobic, 146–162 bpm)** — steady, sustained aerobic work. This is the bread and butter of your fat-burning and aerobic development. Hold cadence 85–92 rpm. Watch HR — if it drifts above 162 bpm, reduce watts by 10W. If it stays under 155 and feels very easy, you can nudge up to 135–141W range. The goal is 40 continuous minutes in Z2, not a watts target.

#### Cool-down
- **10 min @ <90W (Z1)** — gradually reduce resistance. HR should be back under 140 by the time you stop. If you have time, stay on the bike for the full 10 minutes rather than jumping off at 60:00.

**Coach notes:** This is the session that will make the biggest difference to your weight loss and aerobic base over time. Forty minutes of clean Z2 cycling burns fat efficiently, builds the aerobic engine, and — critically — does it without creating the recovery debt that hard sessions do. On a good week, this will start to feel easier. When it does, that means it's working. Ride this one well-fed and hydrated — don't approach Saturday's session on coffee and broken sleep alone if you can help it.
