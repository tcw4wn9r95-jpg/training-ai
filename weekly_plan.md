### Monday 22 June — Aerobic Base Ride

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 22 | **Focus:** Reactivate aerobic system, purely Zone 2

#### Warm-up
- 5 min at 88–106W (Zone 1) — easy spin, find your rhythm, let HR settle. Cadence 85–90 rpm. No pushing.

#### Main set
- 20 min continuous at **106–141W (Zone 2)** — this is the work. HR should drift toward 146–162 bpm but do not chase it. Power is the anchor. Cadence 88–92 rpm. If you feel flat or tired, sit at the bottom of that range (106–115W) and that is perfectly fine.

#### Cool-down
- 5 min at 70–88W — easy spin, HR drops back below 140. Breathe.

**Coach notes:** This is your re-entry session. There is no threshold, no intervals, no heroics. Zone 2 aerobic work is where fat metabolism is trained and aerobic efficiency is built — both directly serve the weight loss goal. Do this right and it is genuinely productive work, even if it feels almost too easy. If the legs feel heavy or sleep was awful last night, dial the power to Zone 1 for the full 30 minutes and call it a win.

### Wednesday 24 June — Threshold Taste (Cycling)

**Sport:** Indoor Cycling
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 28 | **Focus:** Brief threshold stimulus — the "peak" element of this week

#### Warm-up
- 8 min building gradually: first 4 min at 88–106W (Zone 1), next 4 min at 115–135W (low Zone 2), cadence 88–92 rpm. Let HR rise naturally.

#### Main set
- **2 × 6 min at 160–177W (Zone 3–low Zone 4)** — this sits at the top of sweet-spot, nudging threshold. Not a full Z4 blast — a controlled, sustainable effort. HR will likely land in the 163–175 bpm range. Cadence 90+ rpm. Stay smooth, stay seated.
- **3 min easy spin between efforts** at 88–106W — genuine recovery, not a token rest. Let HR drop back below 155 before the second rep starts.

#### Cool-down
- 7 min at 70–95W — full spin-down. HR below 135 before you stop.

**Coach notes:** Two six-minute efforts is enough of a threshold stimulus to fulfil the "peak" intent of this week without demanding recovery you cannot currently give. Focus on holding power steady rather than surging — a consistent 170W for 6 minutes beats a 185W start that fades to 155W. If you are on fewer than 5 hours of sleep, convert this to a straight Zone 2 ride and carry the threshold session to Friday instead.

### Friday 26 June — Lower Body & Core Strength

**Sport:** Strength
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 20 | **Focus:** Leg and core strength endurance — shoulder-safe throughout

#### Warm-up
- 4 min: leg swings (front/back and lateral, 10 each leg), hip circles (10 each direction), 15 bodyweight squats, 10 glute bridges. Slow and deliberate — this is activation, not cardio.

#### Main set
**2 rounds** of the following circuit. Rest 45–60 seconds between exercises, 90 seconds between rounds.

| Exercise | Reps / Time | Load |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squat | 12 reps | 16 kg dumbbell |
| Romanian Deadlift (RDL) | 10 reps each leg | 16 kg dumbbell |
| TRX Split Squat | 10 reps each leg | Bodyweight on TRX |
| Banded Glute Bridge | 20 reps | Resistance band |
| Dead Bug | 8 reps each side | Bodyweight |
| Side Plank | 25 sec each side | Bodyweight |

#### Cool-down
- 5 min: hip flexor stretch (60 sec each side), hamstring stretch on floor (45 sec each side), glute figure-4 stretch (45 sec each side). Slow breathing throughout.

**Coach notes:** Two rounds instead of three — this is intentional given the accumulated fatigue and sleep debt. Quality of movement beats volume every time. On the RDL, keep a soft knee and hinge from the hip — feel the hamstring load before you reverse. The dead bug replaces any pressing or pulling movement to keep the shoulder completely unloaded; it is also one of the best core exercises you can do for cycling posture. If the 16 kg feels heavy on the goblet squat, drop to 11 kg without hesitation.
