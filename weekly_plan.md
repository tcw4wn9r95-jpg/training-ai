### **THURSDAY, JUNE 4 — Strength: Lower Body & Core (Shoulder-Friendly)**
**Sport:** Strength  
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 28 | **Focus:** Strength endurance for legs, glutes, and core; zero overhead work

#### Warm-up (5 min)
- Leg swings (10 each leg, forward/back and lateral)
- Hip circles (10 each direction)
- Bodyweight squats (15 reps, slow)

#### Main set (20 min)
**3 rounds, 60 seconds rest between rounds:**

1. **Goblet Squats** 12 reps × 16 kg (or 22 kg if comfortable)
   - Feet shoulder-width, drive through heels, full depth
   - RPE: 6–7/10 per set

2. **Romanian Deadlift (RDL)** 10 reps × 22 kg
   - Hinge from hips, light knee bend, feel hamstring stretch
   - RPE: 6–7/10 per set

3. **TRX Assisted Split Squat** 8 reps per leg (16 total)
   - Hold TRX at chest height for balance; rear foot elevated on ground
   - RPE: 6–7/10 per set

4. **Glute Bridge** 20 reps (bodyweight or 11 kg dumbbell on hips)
   - Squeeze glutes hard at top, controlled lower
   - RPE: 5–6/10 per set

5. **Plank Hold** 45 seconds
   - Neutral spine, engage core, elbows under shoulders
   - RPE: 6/10

#### Cool-down (5 min)
- Child's pose (30 sec)
- Quad stretch (15 sec each leg)
- Hamstring fold (30 sec)
- Glute figure-four (20 sec each side)

**Coach notes:**  
This circuit targets the whole lower body and core—no pressing, no overhead work. Focus on control and glute activation. 3 rounds should leave you slightly fatigued but not destroyed; this is base-phase strength.
