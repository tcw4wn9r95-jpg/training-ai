## WHEN YOU RETURN — Gentle Reactivation Week

> **Physiological rationale:** After a 1–3 week break, aerobic capacity is largely preserved. Neuromuscular coordination and connective tissue tolerance drop slightly. The right return is **low intensity, moderate duration, no threshold work** — rebuild the habit before rebuilding the load. I'm targeting **70–80 TSS** for the return week, slightly below your recent best of 79 TSS, to respect detraining and sleep disruption (newborn sleep is not recovery sleep).

### Monday — Easy Spin, Reconnect

**Sport:** Cycling Indoor
**Duration:** 30 min | **Planned TSS:** 18 | **Focus:** Reactivate aerobic system, no pressure

#### Warm-up
- 5 min easy spin, 80–90 rpm, **<106W (Z1)** — just turn the legs over, find your rhythm

#### Main set
- 20 min steady **Z2, 106–130W**, HR drifting to 146–158 bpm — conversational effort, no surging
- Keep cadence 85–95 rpm throughout

#### Cool-down
- 5 min easy spin, **<100W** — let HR drop below 130 bpm before stopping

**Coach notes:** This is not a workout, it's a reintroduction. If 30 minutes feels like too much on the day, do 20 and call it a win. The goal is simply to sit on the bike and remember you like this.

### Saturday — Longer Aerobic Ride, Return Anchor

**Sport:** Cycling Indoor
**Duration:** 50 min | **Planned TSS:** 32 | **Focus:** Rebuild weekly long ride habit, aerobic base

#### Warm-up
- 10 min progressive build: start at **<100W**, increase by ~5W every 2 min, arrive at 110W by end of warm-up

#### Main set
- **30 min steady aerobic at 110–135W (Z2)**
  - First 15 min: hold 110–120W — let the body settle
  - Final 15 min: if HR is stable and effort feels easy, nudge to 120–135W
  - HR target: 146–162 bpm throughout — if HR climbs above 165, back off power immediately
  - Cadence: 88–95 rpm

#### Cool-down
- 10 min easy spin: start at 105W, step down to **<90W** by minute 8, spin out to HR below 125 bpm

**Coach notes:** This is the anchor session of the return week — 30 minutes of quality Z2 work is where your aerobic engine rebuilds. The 10-minute warm-up and cool-down aren't padding; they ensure you don't start the main set cold or end it with cardiac drift unresolved. If you feel genuinely strong at the 30-minute mark, hold the pace rather than extending — we're building back, not racing back.
