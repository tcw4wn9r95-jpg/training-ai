### **TUESDAY, JUNE 2** — 30 minutes max
**Session: Strength & Conditioning - Lower Body Focus**

**Objective:** Build strength and power in the lower body to support running and cycling performance. Minimal recovery time before Thursday.

**Structure:**
- **Warmup (5 min):** Dynamic stretching and bodyweight activation (leg swings, glute bridges, lunges).
- **Main Set (20 min):** Circuit format (3 rounds, 90 seconds per round)
  1. **Dumbbell Goblet Squats** (22kg) — 10 reps → targets quadriceps, glutes, core stability
  2. **Single-Leg Deadlifts** (16kg in one hand) — 8 reps each leg → unilateral strength, proprioception
  3. **TRX Hamstring Curls** — 12 reps → posterior chain engagement
  4. **Resistance Band Lateral Walks** (medium band) — 12 reps each direction → hip abductor activation
- **Cooldown (5 min):** Light stretching (quads, hamstrings, glutes, hip flexors). HR should come down to 100–120 bpm naturally.

**Target HR During Main Set:** 150–170 bpm (Z2–Z3 range; strength work typically elevates HR moderately). Not a cardio session, so HR will be secondary.

**RPE:** 6–7/10 (moderate effort, controlled rep execution)

**Coaching Notes:**
- Focus on movement quality over speed. Each rep should be controlled.
- Rest 45–60 seconds between rounds if needed.
- The 22kg dumbbell should feel challenging by the final round; adjust to 16kg if form breaks down.
- This session preserves the 48-hour gap before Thursday's longer session.

### **THURSDAY, JUNE 4** — 60 minutes max
**Session: Threshold Intervals – Cycling (Indoor Preferred)**

**Objective:** Build lactate threshold power and muscular endurance. This is your hardest session of the week.

**Structure:**
- **Warmup (10 min):**
  - 0–5 min: Z1 spinning, 53–106W (50–60% FTP), low cadence (~80 rpm), focus on establishing rhythm
  - 5–10 min: Ramp to Z2, 106–141W (60–80% FTP), cadence 90 rpm

- **Main Set (35 min):** Threshold intervals
  - **4 × 5-minute hard efforts** at Z4 (Threshold), 169–195W (91–105% FTP)
  - **2-minute recovery intervals** between each rep, Z1–Z2, 88–106W (50–60% FTP), easy spinning
  - Pace/cadence: Maintain 95–100 rpm during threshold efforts; 85–90 rpm during recovery

- **Cooldown (15 min):**
  - First 7 min: Z2 easy pedaling, 106–141W, focus on clearing lactate
  - Final 8 min: Z1 very easy spin-down, 53–106W, HR dropping below 130 bpm

**Garmin Alerts:**
- Power zones: Z4 lower = 169W, upper = 195W
- Alert when dropping below 169W or exceeding 195W during intervals
- Record as "Threshold Intervals – Cycling"

**RPE:**
- Threshold efforts: 8–9/10 (hard, sustainable effort; should NOT be all-out)
- Recovery intervals: 2–3/10 (very easy)

**Coaching Notes:**
- **Pacing is critical:** If you cannot hold 169W at the end of rep 4, reduce to 165W for final reps (quality > total power).
- **Cadence:** Do not let cadence drop below 90 rpm during intervals; stay smooth and controlled.
- **Nutrition pre-session:** Light meal 60–90 min before (banana + oats, or similar carb source).
- Post-workout: Consume protein + carbs within 30 min (recovery drink or meal).
- This session will feel hard by rep 3–4; this is expected. Mental toughness matters here.
