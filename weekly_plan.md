### **THURSDAY, JUNE 4** — 60 minutes MAX
**Sport:** Cycling (Indoor)  
**Session Name:** Threshold Power Intervals  
**Goal:** Build FTP-adjacent power and mental toughness in Z4

#### Structure:
- **Warmup (10 min):**  
  - Minutes 0–5: Z1 spin (88–106W, ~55–60% FTP), easy cadence 90 rpm
  - Minutes 5–10: Gradual ramp to Z2 (106–141W, 60–75% FTP), cadence 95 rpm
  
- **Main Set (35 min):** 5 × 4-minute threshold efforts
  - **Effort (4 min):** Z4 power, 169–195W (91–105% FTP), cadence 95–105 rpm
  - **Recovery (3 min):** Z1, 88–106W, cadence 90 rpm (active recovery)
  - Repeat 5 times (total: 20 min effort + 15 min recovery)

- **Cooldown (15 min):**  
  - Minutes 0–10: Easy Z1 spin, 88–106W, bring breathing under control
  - Minutes 10–15: Very light Z1 pedaling, 53–88W, depress HR to <130 bpm

#### Garmin Alerts:
- Set power alerts: lower 169W, upper 195W for main efforts
- Monitor cadence; alert if drops below 90 rpm (form cue)
- Record TSS (expect ~120–140 TSS for this session)

#### Coaching Notes:
- **Pacing strategy:** The first two 4-min blocks should feel hard but sustainable. By block 3, mental focus is critical. Blocks 4–5, you're fighting the mental game—expect discomfort.
- Stay seated; avoid standing until final 30 sec of each effort if possible (preserves power).
- Hydration: sip water during 3-min recovery intervals.
- RPE: 8–9/10 during efforts; 2/10 during recovery
