### **TUESDAY, JUNE 2 — Strength & Conditioning Circuit** ⏱️ 30 minutes
**Sport:** Strength | **Equipment:** Dumbbells (11kg, 16kg), TRX, resistance bands | **RPE:** 6/10

#### Structure
- **Warmup (5 min):** Arm circles, leg swings, band pull-aparts, bodyweight squats. HR should reach ~110–120 bpm.
- **Main Circuit (20 min):** 4 rounds × 5 minutes each round
  - **Round 1–4:** Perform the following in a circuit (minimal rest between exercises):
    1. TRX rows × 8 reps (challenging tempo, 3s eccentric)
    2. Dumbbell goblet squats (16kg) × 10 reps
    3. Resistance band chest press × 12 reps
    4. Single-leg Romanian deadlifts (11kg) × 8/side
    5. TRX hamstring curls × 8 reps
  - Rest 60–90 seconds between rounds; maintain HR 130–150 bpm during circuit.
- **Cooldown (5 min):** Static stretching (hamstrings, quads, shoulders, chest). HR drops to <120 bpm.

#### Garmin Alerts
- Record as "Strength" in Garmin Connect
- Monitor HR to keep intensity controlled (not max effort, controlled effort)

#### Coaching Notes
- This session builds lower-body stability and upper-body pulling strength.
- Tempo is key: control the eccentric (lowering) phase for 3 seconds on rows and deadlifts.
- If HR stays below 130 bpm during the circuit, increase reps or reduce rest.

### **THURSDAY, JUNE 4 — Threshold Cycling (Indoor)** ⏱️ 60 minutes
**Sport:** Cycling (Indoor) | **Zone:** Z3–Z4 Threshold | **RPE:** 7–8/10

#### Structure
- **Warmup (10 min):** 
  - 0–3 min: Spin easy, Z1, 88–106W
  - 3–7 min: Build to Z2, 106–141W
  - 7–10 min: 2× 30-second ramps to Z3 (142–168W), recover to Z2 between ramps
- **Main Set (35 min):**
  - 3× 8-minute threshold efforts at Z4, 169–195W (92–110% FTP), with 3-minute Z2 recovery (106–141W) between efforts
  - Target: Hold steady power on each interval; do not spike above 195W at the start.
- **Cooldown (15 min):**
  - 0–5 min: Spin easy Z1, 88–106W
  - 5–10 min: Very easy Z1, 53–88W
  - 10–15 min: Final 5 min at Z1, focus on smooth cadence (90–95 rpm)

#### Garmin Alerts
- Set power zones: Z1 <106W, Z2 106–141W, Z3 142–168W, Z4 169–195W
- Alert on power dips below 160W during threshold intervals (early warning of fatigue)
- Record as "Threshold Intervals" or "Sweet Spot" in Garmin

#### Coaching Notes
- This is your hardest session of the week. Threshold work improves lactate clearance and aerobic power.
- **Mental focus:** Each 8-minute block is a mini-race. Break it into 2×4-min or 4×2-min if needed.
- Cadence 88–95 rpm; avoid grinding or spinning too fast (wasted energy).
- If power drops >10W in final 2 min of an interval, extend recovery to 4 min before the next.
- Hydration: 500 mL water during session; light snack (banana or toast) 30 min post-ride.
